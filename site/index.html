CalWallpaper

Your calendar events and tasks, drawn calmly on your Android home and lock screen.

Download for Android

Free and open source. Android 8.0 or newer.

Features

  • Agenda on your wallpaperHome and lock screen, with presets that avoid the clock.
  • Your sourcesAny calendar synced to your phone (including Google Calendar), local tasks, and Todoist.
  • Tap to openHome-screen widgets and wallpaper taps open the right day or task in the source app.
  • Private by defaultNo account, no analytics, no ads. Privacy mode hides titles while the phone is locked.
  • Light on batteryRedraws only when something changes. No timers, no background service.

Status

Early development. Releases are published on GitHub; Google Play is planned.
