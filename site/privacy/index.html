Privacy policy

CalWallpaper does not collect, share or sell your data. Everything it shows is read and drawn on your phone.

Effective 2026-10-17. Applies to CalWallpaper for Android (io.github.nabin47.calwallpaper), from Google Play or from the GitHub releases.

Who is responsible

CalWallpaper is an open-source app published by its developer, GitHub user nabin47. It has no company, server or backend behind it. For questions or requests about privacy, see Contact.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • Your calendar events and tasks never leave your phone, except that Todoist, if you connect it, is contacted directly to fetch your own tasks.
  • The developer receives no data from the app, ever.

Calendar

If you grant the calendar permission (READ_CALENDAR), CalWallpaper reads events from the calendars stored on your phone, including accounts such as Google Calendar that your phone already syncs. It uses them only to draw your wallpaper, widgets and the preview in the app. Access is read-only: the app never changes or deletes events, and never sends them anywhere. You can revoke the permission at any time in Android's settings.

Your local task list

Tasks you add in the app, its Quick Settings tile or its shortcut are stored in a database on your phone. They are never uploaded by the app.

Todoist (optional)

If you connect Todoist, you paste a personal API token from your Todoist settings. The token is encrypted with a key kept in your phone's Android Keystore, is never shown again, and is used only to fetch your tasks and projects directly from Todoist (api.todoist.com) over HTTPS. The app only reads from Todoist; it never creates, changes or deletes anything there. A copy of your open, dated tasks is kept on your phone so the wallpaper works offline. Disconnecting Todoist in the app deletes the token and that copy. Your use of Todoist is governed by Doist's privacy policy.

Opening other apps

When you tap a day, event or task on the home screen or in a widget, CalWallpaper asks Android to open it in the matching app: your calendar app, the Todoist app, or, if that app is missing, the page in your web browser. Only what is needed to open that item is passed (for example the event's id and time, or a Todoist link). What the other app does is governed by its own privacy policy.

Network

The app makes network requests only to Todoist, and only if you connect it. All connections use HTTPS; unencrypted connections are blocked. There are no other servers, so there is nothing to send data to.

Data stored on your phone

  • Settings: your appearance, layout and source choices.
  • Wallpaper cache: what the wallpaper last showed, so it can appear after a restart before you unlock the phone. When privacy mode is on, it contains no event or task titles.
  • Local tasks, and the Todoist token and offline copy described above.
  • Diagnostics: draw times and memory use, shown only in the app's settings. Never sent.

Backups

If you use Android's own backup (for example to your Google account), it may include your settings and your local tasks, under your control and Google's terms. It never includes the wallpaper cache, the Todoist copy or the Todoist token.

Permissions

  • READ_CALENDAR: show your events (asked only when you choose to, with an explanation).
  • INTERNET: Todoist, only if you connect it.
  • SET_WALLPAPER, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: battery saver mode sets a still image of your agenda and refreshes it after a restart; the widgets also refresh after a restart.
  • WAKE_LOCK, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE: added by Android's WorkManager library, which keeps widgets and the Todoist sync up to date.

None of these, other than the calendar, gives access to your personal data. Android grants them without asking.

Your choices and deleting data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Turn off privacy-sensitive features at any time, disconnect Todoist in the app, clear the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstall the app to delete all of it. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, export or delete from us.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page, with a new effective date, and in the source repository, where every past version is kept.
