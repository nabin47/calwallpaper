Privacy policy

CalWallpaper does not collect, share or sell your data.

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Calendar access

If you grant the calendar permission, CalWallpaper reads events from your phone's calendar storage to draw them on your wallpaper and widgets. This happens only on your device. Events are never sent anywhere.

Todoist

If you connect Todoist, you paste a personal API token. The token is stored encrypted on your device, excluded from backups, and used only to fetch your tasks directly from Todoist over HTTPS. A copy of your open, dated tasks is kept on your device so the wallpaper works offline; it is not backed up, and disconnecting Todoist in the app deletes it together with the token. Your use of Todoist is covered by Todoist's own privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting service. The app has no server. The only network requests go to services you turn on yourself (for example Todoist).

Data on your device

Settings and a cache of what is shown on the wallpaper are stored locally. The cache lets the wallpaper appear right after a restart, before you unlock the phone; when privacy mode is on, it contains no event or task titles. Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Android's own backup may include your settings, but never the cache and never your Todoist token.

Wallpaper permissions

In battery saver mode, CalWallpaper sets a picture of your agenda as your wallpaper and refreshes it after a restart. Android grants the two permissions this needs without asking; they give no access to your data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the source repository. Questions: open an issue at github.com/nabin47/calwallpaper/issues.
