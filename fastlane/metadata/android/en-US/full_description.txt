CalWallpaper draws your calendar events and tasks on your home and lock-screen wallpaper, calmly and legibly.

• Works with any calendar synced to your phone, including Google Calendar
• Local tasks and Todoist
• Home-screen widgets that open the right day or task
• Privacy mode hides titles while your phone is locked
• Layout presets that keep clear of the lock-screen clock
• No account, no ads, no analytics. Your data stays on your device
• Light on battery: redraws only when something changes
